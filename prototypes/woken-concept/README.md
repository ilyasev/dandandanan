# woken-concept — «Тот, кто просыпается»

**Status:** concluded — PIVOT (see REPORT.md)

## Hypothesis
If the player passes out as the businessman and wakes up as the addict, they want to find the
link between the two lives. True if, after the one pair of scenes, the player unprompted names a
mirror detail (the watch stopped at 4:17 ↔ the pawn ticket "В. от деда", due 17.04), states a
theory (one person or two) and wants to continue. False if they say "confusing" or "just two
separate stories".

## How to run
Open this folder (`prototypes/woken-concept/`) as its own project in Godot 4.7.2, press F5. Or:

```
"E:/ilya/games/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Godot Engine/godot.windows.opt.tools.64.exe" --path prototypes/woken-concept --windowed --resolution 1280x720
```

About 4–5 minutes. Click "Далее" and the choices. The final answer is printed to the console as
`[PROTOTYPE] answer=…`.

## What is in it
- Businessman scene (blue): one choice about the factory, the watch at 4:17, drink or endure (both lead to blackout)
- Blackout fade and palette switch to ochre
- Addict scene: the pawn ticket, the businessman's voice РАСЧЁТ speaks once in the addict's head
- End screen: "Один это человек или двое?"

## Findings
Switch works and the link is noticed; the watch detail is too direct; visual-novel form and named inner voices rejected. Pivot: walkable 2D isometric scenes. Full findings in REPORT.md.
